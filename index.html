Our Philosophy

A Thoughtful Space Designed for Quiet Rejuvenation

Located on the 3rd Floor of the RNR Building in Kattigenahalli, Luvai Unisex Salon was founded with a singular purpose: to bring high-calibre hair craftsmanship and aesthetic care into an atmosphere of quiet calm.

Guided by our belief in "Beauty for Everyone," our team combines skilled hands with genuine, friendly hospitality. We avoid rushed appointments and crowded spaces, offering clients across Yelahanka and the REVA University neighbourhood a clean, peaceful environment to unwind and look their best.
